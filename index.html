02 · Research

Learning when incentives matter

My central research question is how to design learning systems that remain effective when participants are strategic, information is incomplete, and the system must learn while making decisions.

Primary research direction

Mechanism Design in Machine Learning

I study incentive structures in machine-learning environments: how the behavior of agents changes what a learner observes, what can be inferred from data, and how a platform should design payments, contracts, or allocation rules to obtain desirable outcomes.

Game Theory Mechanism Design Incentives Strategic ML
01

Online Learning & Bandits

Exploration–exploitation trade-offs, partial feedback, confidence-based learning, and finite-sample guarantees for sequential decision problems.

UCB Best-arm Identification Regret
02

LLM Routing & Contracts

Learning how a platform can induce the right model through a contract when model qualities are initially unknown and actions are selected by a self-interested agent.

LLMs Contracts Principal–Agent
03

Incentive-aware AI Systems

Intelligent, fair, and efficient learning mechanisms connecting theoretical analysis with data-driven decision systems.

AI Optimization Decision Systems
04

Federated Learning & Incentives

Strategic participation, free-riding, fairness, privacy, and robust aggregation in decentralized learning systems with heterogeneous clients.

Federated Learning Game Theory Fairness
