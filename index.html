02 · Research

Learning when incentives matter.

My central research question is how to design learning systems that remain effective when participants are strategic, information is incomplete, and the system must learn while making decisions.

PRIMARY RESEARCH DIRECTION

Mechanism Design in Machine Learning

I study incentive structures in machine-learning environments: how the behavior of agents changes what a learner observes, what can be inferred from data, and how a platform should design payments, contracts, or allocation rules to obtain desirable outcomes.

GAME THEORY MECHANISM DESIGN INCENTIVES STRATEGIC ML
01

Online Learning & Bandits

Exploration–exploitation trade-offs, partial feedback, confidence-based learning, and finite-sample guarantees for sequential decision problems.

UCBBEST-ARM IDENTIFICATIONREGRET
02

LLM Routing & Contracts

Learning how a platform can induce the right model through a contract when model qualities are initially unknown and actions are selected by a self-interested agent rather than directly by the platform.

LLMsCONTRACTSPRINCIPAL–AGENT
03

Incentive-aware AI Systems

More broadly, I am interested in intelligent, fair, and efficient learning mechanisms that connect theoretical analysis with data-driven decision systems.

AIOPTIMIZATIONDECISION SYSTEMS
04

Federated Learning & Incentives

Strategic participation, free-riding, fairness, privacy, and robust aggregation in decentralized learning systems where clients can have heterogeneous objectives and behavior.

06 · About

From mathematics to strategic AI.

My academic path has moved through mathematics, statistics, data science, and computer science. This combination has shaped my interest in problems where mathematical structure and learning interact with economic incentives and strategic behavior.

My core technical background is in Machine Learning and Deep Learning, with interests in algorithm design, optimization, and data-driven decision systems. I am particularly interested in building intelligent mechanisms that connect theoretical guarantees with practical AI systems.

Before beginning my Ph.D., I was a visiting undergraduate scholar at IIT Gandhinagar in summer 2024 and a visiting postgraduate scholar at NIELIT Patna in spring 2025.
